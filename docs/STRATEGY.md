# Studex Eswatini Strategy

Status: Working strategy
Last updated: 2026-11-06
Decision owner: Tumelo
Field lead: Keenan

## Strategic objective

Establish one credible, locally anchored Studex Eswatini operating node that
turns real institutional and enterprise problems into paid, measurable pilots.
The node combines a private Business Ghost, specialist agents, a governed client
workspace, local implementation capacity, and a route to scaled infrastructure
only when demand is proven.

This is not a promise to deploy a national data centre, launch 100 agent seats,
or sign an exclusive distributor on a fixed date. Those remain gated outcomes.

## Positioning

Studex is a forward-deployed development and technology partner. The operating
loop is:

`Discover -> Qualify -> Scope -> Prove -> Contract -> Localise -> Scale`

The offer is a managed AI operating environment, not a bundle of disconnected
agents and not speculative infrastructure.

## Initial client offer

### Eswatini Discovery Sprint

A four-week paid engagement that produces:

1. A verified opportunity and stakeholder map.
2. A governed Business Ghost containing only approved source material.
3. One workflow prototype tied to a measurable operational problem.
4. A security, data-governance, connectivity, and model-cost assessment.
5. A decision pack for a 60-day pilot, including scope, owner, budget route,
   acceptance measures, risks, and local capability plan.

### Sixty-day pilot

The first pilot should be selected only after discovery. Candidate families are:

- sovereign/private knowledge workflows with RSTP or the National Data Centre;
- agriculture and field-evidence workflows with one sugar-sector operator;
- an SME operating cohort through UNESWA and SEDCO;
- a safety-governed mining knowledge workflow after specialist review.

The pilot includes a tenant-isolated workspace, private corpus, role-based agent
access, audit log, usage budget, human approvals, backups, and a handover pack.

### Scale options

- additional workflows and agent seats;
- dedicated client VM or managed container;
- local implementation and train-the-trainer programme;
- private or edge inference where data, latency, or continuity requires it;
- physical infrastructure feasibility only after contracted demand, site,
  power, connectivity, regulatory, and financing gates are satisfied.

## Commercial model

Use the historical Tier D terms only as a pricing hypothesis:

- country VM reference specification: 8 vCPU / 32 GB RAM;
- historical distribution fee: R24,000 per year;
- historical Studex royalty: 20% of country revenue.

These figures are not approved client pricing. Before quoting, build a costed
offer covering cloud hosting, storage, backups, model usage, support, security,
implementation, training, taxes, and partner margin. Do not appoint an exclusive
distribution partner before the first paid pilot and due diligence.

## Priority opportunity sequence

1. RSTP / National Data Centre: sovereign/private AI sandbox and skills transfer.
2. Ubombo Sugar or Royal Eswatini Sugar Corporation: one field-to-decision
   workflow, not both simultaneously.
3. UNESWA + SEDCO: local implementer and SME cohort.
4. Maloma Colliery: approved-document safety and maintenance knowledge pilot;
   no autonomous equipment control.
5. MTN Eswatini: connectivity and distribution scale after a working proof.

An organisation becomes qualified only when all five gates are evidenced:

1. named internal sponsor;
2. measurable problem;
3. decision-maker or procurement route;
4. plausible budget or funded programme;
5. dated next step.

## Platform strategy

### Per-client layer

- isolated Business Ghost and approved private corpus;
- client identity, objectives, opportunities, and CRM context;
- agent roster, permissions, and workflow library;
- audit/event log, backups, and export procedure;
- model routing, token budget, and cost controls;
- dashboard, notification rules, and human approval queue.

### Shared Studex layer

- model gateway and provider routing;
- research and engineering workers;
- observability, health monitoring, and FinOps;
- reusable workflow templates;
- Tailscale/private administration and a protected client web surface;
- specialist agents loaded on demand rather than permanently running every role.

The minimum proof is:

`human task -> Buzz group -> lead agent -> specialist -> result saved ->`
`Business Ghost updated -> dashboard event -> human notification`

No agent is considered live until this workflow passes with evidence.

The detailed deployment and client-access design is in
`docs/DEPLOYMENT_PLAN.md`.

## Partner strategy

Huawei may be evaluated for servers, storage, networking, edge compute, and
cloud services. Alibaba Cloud and ART Engineering may be evaluated as alternate
or complementary providers. Every partner claim must carry one of these states:

- candidate;
- invited;
- meeting held;
- technical evaluation;
- proposal received;
- approved supplier;
- contracted partner.

Do not describe a candidate or meeting participant as a partner. Keep the full
Huawei and Black Cloud investment materials outside this country repository;
link only the Eswatini-relevant decision or technical evidence.

## Governance

Human approval is required before external outreach, public claims, spending,
travel, contracts, production deployment, access changes, or regulated actions.

Maintain explicit labels for:

- verified fact;
- user-provided claim;
- hypothesis;
- proposal;
- decision;
- commitment.

Government, mining, telecom, payments, lending, drones, identity, surveillance,
and data-centre claims require appropriate legal, regulatory, safety, privacy,
and commercial diligence.

## Go / pivot / hold

GO when there is a named sponsor, measurable problem, usable data, approved
security boundary, budget route, local owner, and successful staged proof.

PIVOT when the sponsor exists but the proposed workflow, provider, or vertical
cannot meet data, budget, regulatory, or value requirements.

HOLD capital deployment when there is no contracted demand, site feasibility,
power/connectivity assessment, regulatory route, or financing evidence.
