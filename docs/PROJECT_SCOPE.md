# Eswatini Programme Scope

## Outcome

Build a dedicated, locally testable operating environment for Keenan and the
Studex Eswatini programme. It must later be deployable as
`SGM-ESWATINI-KEENAN-01` without redesigning the system.

## Core operating areas

- Country command and Business Ghost
- Opportunity and localisation strategy
- Government and institutional stakeholder management
- Mining and artisanal-miner modernisation
- Data-centre and infrastructure opportunities
- Agriculture and drone-enabled services
- Telecom opportunities and feasibility
- Commercial partnerships and development finance
- Research, market intelligence, risk, and compliance
- Local SME, skills, training, and employment development

## Dashboard records

Every record needs an owner, source, confidence level, status, next action, due
date, linked documents, and recent agent activity.

Required record types:

- Opportunities
- Organisations and companies
- Government and institutional stakeholders
- People and relationships
- Meetings and commitments
- Pilots and projects
- Decisions and approvals
- Risks and regulatory questions
- Documents and research
- Agent tasks, runs, messages, and health events

## Delivery stages

1. Inventory existing Eswatini assets and agents.
2. Publish the current-state architecture and country opportunity map.
3. Make the branded country dashboard visible.
4. Connect the existing Buzz agent group without duplicating identities.
5. Complete one end-to-end agent workflow.
6. Add persistent memory and notification delivery.
7. Package an Orgo-ready deployment manifest; do not deploy without approval.

## Acceptance workflow

Human task -> Buzz group -> assigned lead agent -> specialist collaboration ->
tool action -> result saved -> Business Ghost updated -> dashboard refreshed ->
notification delivered -> heartbeat healthy.

An agent is not operational merely because its process is running.

## Boundaries

Telecom, payments, currency, mining, healthcare/aesthetics, government work, and
other regulated areas remain proposals until legal, technical, commercial, and
regulatory diligence is complete. The system must distinguish verified facts,
working hypotheses, proposals, commitments, and approvals.
