Last updated: 2026-09-09
Owner: Rio (plan). Approver: Tumelo. Field: Keenan.

Horizon: 10 Sep–23 Sep 2026 (two weeks). This is Days 1–14 of the
qualify-and-specify window, not a rewrite of the 90-day framework.

## North star for these two weeks

Leave bootstrap with three things that are true on disk:

1. A source catalogue of existing Eswatini material, in this repo.
2. A prospect tracker with qualification gates, not a slogan list.
3. A confirmed or cancelled 28 Sep–3 Oct travel/programme window.

Everything else waits if it fights those three.

## Still true (do not re-litigate)

- Studex is a forward-deployed development and technology partner, not a
  disconnected AI/drone/mining vendor.
- First-wave sequence: RSTP/NDC → Ubombo or RES → UNESWA + SEDCO → Maloma → MTN.
- Three pilot families: sovereign local-AI; agriculture/drone evidence;
  UNESWA/SEDCO capability.
- Draft ≠ commitment. No named sponsor, meeting, budget, or endorsement
  is confirmed until Keenan and Tumelo say so.
- 28 Sep–3 Oct on the mission calendar is unconfirmed (possible Switzerland copy).
